# Dr. Okezie Sampson Chukwuemeka

### Chief Research Fellow | Meteorologist | Agrometeorologist | Climate & Environmental Researcher

Welcome to my professional research portfolio.

I am a **Chief Research Fellow and Meteorologist at the National Root Crops Research Institute (NRCRI), Umudike**, with research interests spanning **agrometeorology, climate change, crop-weather modelling, spatial analysis, environmental systems, and agricultural decision support**.

My work combines atmospheric science, environmental research, data analysis, spatial technologies, and field-based observations to understand climate–environment–agriculture interactions and support evidence-based decision-making.

---

## About Me

I am a researcher with a background in **Physics, Engineering Physics, Meteorology and Agrometeorology**, working at the intersection of climate, agriculture, environmental systems and data-driven research.

My professional experience includes research, meteorological observations, agricultural weather analysis, field data collection, spatial analysis, scientific communication and research project development.

I am particularly interested in applying quantitative and spatial approaches to understand environmental processes and their implications for agriculture and sustainable development.

## Selected Research & Projects

### Lake Chad Sustainability Research

Participated in research activities addressing the sustainability of agrarian societies within the Lake Chad Basin.

**1st International Symposium for Sustainability of Agrarian Societies in the Lake Chad Basin**  
Maiduguri, Nigeria — May 2024

Research activities included field data collection and assessment of perceptions and impacts associated with the changing Lake Chad environment.

A total of **967 responses** were collected using **KoBoToolbox**.

### Perceived Impact of Shrinking Lake Chad on Agrarian Communities

Research work examining how changes associated with Lake Chad are perceived by agrarian communities and their implications for livelihoods and agricultural systems.

### Sustainability Lake Chad Project Group

Participated in activities of the Sustainability Lake Chad Project Group, including completion of Phase 1 activities and participation in the **Bayes2026 Workshop**.

### Climate and Meteorological Data Research

Research involving long-term meteorological observations from **NRCRI Umudike**, including datasets extending back to **1921**.

Such datasets provide an important basis for examining long-term weather and climate variability and their implications for agricultural systems.

### Gender-Transformative Methane Reduction Research

Contributed to development of a concept/proposal addressing **gender-transformative methane reduction** under an ACCF call.

The proposal included an estimated value of **$895,000**, with approximately **$100,500 counterpart contribution**.
