## 👋 About Me

I am **Dr. Okezie Sampson Chukwuemeka**, a Nigerian researcher and **Principal Research Fellow / Meteorologist** at the **National Root Crops Research Institute (NRCRI), Umudike**.

My work sits at the intersection of **meteorology, agrometeorology, climate change, environmental science, spatial analysis, data analysis, and applied physics**.

I combine scientific research, field data collection, quantitative analysis, modelling, GIS, and scientific communication to investigate environmental and agricultural problems and support evidence-based decision-making.

## 🌍 Selected Research & Projects

### Lake Chad Basin Research

Research on the environmental and socioeconomic implications of changes in Lake Chad and their effects on agrarian communities.

One related research work is:

> **Perceived Impact of Shrinking Lake Chad on Agrarian Communities**

The work involved field data collection, community-level research and quantitative analysis.

---

### 🌦️ Long-Term Meteorological Data

Research and analysis involving the historical meteorological archive of NRCRI Umudike.

The archive contains observations extending back to **1921**, providing an important basis for studying:

- Climate variability
- Long-term weather patterns
- Agricultural climate risks
- Climate trends
- Weather-dependent agricultural decision-making
